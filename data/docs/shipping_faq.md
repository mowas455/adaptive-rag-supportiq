# NexCart Shipping FAQ

## Which shipping speeds do you offer?

Standard Ground is 5–7 business days after the order leaves the warehouse. Economy is 7–10 business days and is the default for orders under $35. Expedited is 2–3 business days. Overnight is next business day if the order is placed before 1:00 p.m. Eastern and in stock at the New Jersey or Texas fulfillment center. NexCart Plus members get free Standard Ground on all orders over $15 and one free Expedited upgrade per calendar month.

Cut-off times: warehouse processing ends at 1:00 p.m. Eastern on weekdays. Orders placed after cut-off, or on Saturday/Sunday, ship the next business day. Federal holidays pause both processing and transit clocks.

## When will my order ship?

Most in-stock items ship within 24 hours on business days. Pre-order items show an estimated ship week on the product page. If a line item is backordered, we split-ship available items immediately and send the remainder when restocked unless you chose "Ship complete" at checkout. Split shipments do not add extra shipping fees for Plus members; other customers pay one additional Standard Ground fee ($4.99) for the second package.

You will receive a tracking email when the label is created. Tracking may sit on "Label created" for up to 24 hours before the first scan. That is normal.

## Do you ship to PO boxes, APO/FPO, and Alaska/Hawaii?

PO boxes accept Standard Ground and Economy only; Expedited and Overnight cannot be used. APO/FPO and U.S. territories ship via USPS and typically take 10–18 business days. Alaska and Hawaii orders have a $12.00 remote surcharge unless you have Plus. We do not offer Overnight to Alaska, Hawaii, or territories.

## International shipping

NexCart currently ships from the U.S. catalog to Canada. Canadian orders incur duties and GST collected at checkout (DDP). Transit is 6–12 business days. We do not ship the NexCart Basics electrical line to Canada because of certification differences. Mexico and EU shipping are on the 2026 roadmap and are not available yet.

## Missing, stolen, or delayed packages

If tracking shows delivered but you cannot find the package, wait 24 hours and check with neighbors and building staff. After 24 hours, open a **Delivery issue** ticket. We file a carrier trace. If the trace does not locate the package within 5 business days, we reship or refund at your choice.

Delays displayed by the carrier (weather, customs, facility backlog) are outside NexCart control. We will not refund shipping for weather delays, but we will extend the return window as described in the Return Policy.

Packages marked "exception – undeliverable" are usually address issues. Confirm the shipping address in **Orders**. If the address was wrong, we can redirect before the package is returned to the warehouse; after it returns, we can reship with a new label at your expense.

## Shipping restrictions

We cannot ship lithium batteries, aerosols, or perfume by air (Expedited/Overnight). Those items automatically downgrade to Ground. Hazardous items show a "Ground only" badge on the product page.

Signature on delivery is required for orders over $400 and for all jewelry SKUs. You can add signature for $4.50 on smaller orders in checkout.

## Changing an address after purchase

You can edit the shipping address from **Orders → Change address** until the status is "Packed". After that, contact support immediately; we will try a carrier intercept. Intercepts succeed about 60% of the time and take 1–3 days. If intercept fails, complete the original delivery and start a return, or refuse the package at the door.
