# NexCart GlowBar LED Light Strip — Product Manual (Excerpt)

Model: NC-GB-2M (2 meter) and NC-GB-5M (5 meter). Intended for indoor dry locations only. Read this excerpt before installation. The full PDF in the box covers electrical diagrams; this page covers setup, app control, and care that support agents answer most often.

## What's in the box

- LED strip on a reel with 3M adhesive backing
- 24W USB-C power adapter and 1.5 m power cable
- Controller brick with a physical power button
- Two spare adhesive clips and an alcohol wipe
- Quick-start card with Matter pairing QR

The 5 meter kit includes a second controller if you cut the strip into two zones. Do not chain two 5 meter kits on one adapter.

## Installation

Unplug the adapter during install. Clean the mounting surface with the wipe and let it dry. Cold rooms (below 50°F / 10°C) reduce adhesive tack; warm the strip in your hands for a minute. Press firmly for 10 seconds every 20 cm. Avoid hanging the strip from a single clip at a corner; use two clips so the data line is not pinched.

You may cut the strip only on the marked copper scissor lines (every 10 cm). Cutting elsewhere permanently damages that segment. After a cut, seal the exposed end with the included silicone cap. To add a second zone, attach the extra controller on a cut section; each zone appears separately in the app.

Do not install behind a TV so tightly that the strip cannot dissipate heat. Leave 2 mm of air. Do not put GlowBar in bathrooms, aquariums, or outdoors. IP20 rating means dust protection only, no water resistance.

## First-time app setup

1. Plug in the adapter. The strip should show a slow white breathing animation.
2. Open the NexCart app (iOS 16+ / Android 12+) and tap **Add device → GlowBar**.
3. Join the temporary "GlowBar-XXXX" Wi-Fi network when prompted, then select your home 2.4 GHz network. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported.
4. Name the device and assign a room. Optional: tap **Works with** to expose the strip to Apple Home, Google Home, or Alexa via Matter.

If the breathing animation never starts, try a different outlet and confirm the USB-C connector clicks into the controller. A solid red LED on the controller means the adapter is under-powered; swap to the included 24W brick.

## Controls and scenes

The physical button on the controller: one press toggles on/off, double-press cycles Warm / Cool / RGB party, hold 5 seconds resets Wi-Fi without erasing scenes.

In the app you can set brightness 1–100%, color temperature 2700K–6500K, and RGB. Scenes sync across GlowBar devices in the same room. Schedules use the phone's local timezone. Adaptive brightness (ambient light) requires granting camera permission; the camera is used only while the toggle is on and no frames leave the device.

Music reactive mode samples the phone microphone. There is no onboard mic on the strip.

## Care and safety

Unplug before wiping. Use a dry microfiber cloth. Do not bend the strip tighter than a 2 cm radius. If a segment fails, that section can be cut out and the remaining strip reused with a new controller (sold separately).

The adapter will feel warm. If it is too hot to hold, unplug and contact support; that is not normal. Children should not pull on the cable. This product is not a toy.

Firmware updates install overnight when the strip is on Wi-Fi and idle. Do not unplug during an update (the app shows a yellow banner). Firmware 1.8 added Matter; 1.9 improved flicker on low dim levels.

## Specifications

Power: 24W max. Voltage: 5V USB-C. Lifetime: 25,000 hours at 70% brightness. Operating temperature: 32–95°F (0–35°C). Warranty: 12-month NexCart Basics limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, not adhesive failure on poorly prepared surfaces or water damage.
