# NexCart Account & Billing FAQ

## How do I create or recover an account?

You can check out as a guest, but order history, returns, and Plus benefits require an account. Sign up with email or a passkey. We do not support social logins other than Sign in with Apple.

If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot password**. Reset links expire in 30 minutes. After five failed password attempts, the account locks for 15 minutes. Passkey users can add a backup email in **Security**. If you lost both the passkey device and email access, contact support with a photo of a government ID matching the billing name; we will not reset accounts over chat without that check.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and NexCart Store Card. Apple Pay and Google Pay are available in the mobile app and on Safari/Chrome. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

Cards are stored by our processor (not on NexCart servers) as tokens. You can keep up to five cards. The default card is charged for Plus membership renewals and for any split-shipment remainder.

Failed charges: we retry a declined card after 24 hours and again after 72 hours. If the third attempt fails, the pending order is cancelled and inventory is released. You will get an email after each attempt.

## Invoices, receipts, and tax

Receipts are emailed at shipment, not at purchase, so the receipt matches what actually shipped. Download PDFs from **Orders → Invoice**. For business purchases, add a company name and tax ID under **Billing → Business profile** before you order; we cannot rewrite an invoice after the fact except to correct a legal name mismatch.

Sales tax is charged based on the shipping address. NexCart collects tax in all U.S. states where we have nexus. Tax-exempt organizations must upload a valid certificate; approval takes 1–2 business days. Exemption applies to future orders only.

## NexCart Plus billing

Plus is $79 per year or $9 per month. Annual plans auto-renew 7 days before the anniversary. Monthly plans renew on the same calendar day each month. You can cancel anytime in **Account → Plus**; access continues through the paid period. We do not prorate annual cancellations except within the first 14 days of a new annual subscription.

Plus includes free Standard Ground, one Expedited upgrade per month, early access to Basics drops, and a 5% store-credit bonus on change-of-mind returns. Sharing a Plus login across households violates the terms and can result in membership revocation without refund.

## Refunds to the original payment method

Refunds follow the Return Policy timeline (typically 3–5 business days after warehouse inspection). Store credit is instant. Bank posting times vary: debit cards 3–7 days, credit cards up to one billing cycle. We cannot "push" a faster bank post.

If you paid with Apple Pay, the refund returns to the card inside the Apple Pay wallet, not to Apple Cash.

## Chargebacks

If you open a chargeback with your bank while a return is in progress, we pause the return refund to avoid double recovery. Contact support before disputing a charge. Friendly fraud (claiming non-receipt after tracking shows a signature) may result in account closure.

## Gift cards and promotional credit

Gift cards do not expire. Promotional credit (for example, a $10 apology credit) expires 90 days after issue and cannot be combined with some sitewide coupon codes. Credits apply after coupons, before tax. Gift cards are not reloadable and cannot be redeemed for Plus membership.

## Closing your account

Request closure in **Privacy → Delete account**. We retain order records for 7 years for tax and fraud reasons but delete marketing profiles within 30 days. You cannot reopen a deleted account; you must create a new one with a different email.
